From picture to printed part

Three steps, all on your own computer.

  1. Add a picture

    A drawing, a render or a photo. If it has a background, a few clicks take it out.

  2. Trace it

    The outline and the line art come out as two SVGs that sit exactly on top of each other.

  3. Print it

    One STL for the figure, base included, and one for the stand. Open them in any slicer.

Questions

Something not covered here? Write to standeemaker.support@gmail.com.

What can I do with the trial?
Everything the full program does. Files come out at full size and full quality, with no watermark. The limits are one new subject every seven days, and fourteen days after the first run the program stops writing files; stands are not limited. What you make during the trial is for trying the program out. Buy a licence and it covers what you have already made.
Do I need an internet connection?
Once, to activate a licence key. After that the program reconfirms the key about once a month when a connection happens to be there, and keeps working offline when it is not. Images, SVGs and STLs never leave your computer.
How many computers can I install it on?
Two, owned or controlled by you. To move the licence, release it from inside the program once that activation is 30 days old, or write to us and we release it sooner, for example if a computer broke. In the EU you can also pass the whole licence on to someone else.
What printer do I need?
The one you have. Standee Maker writes STL files that open in your slicer like any other model. The defaults suit an FDM printer with a 0.4 mm nozzle, which is where the minimum line width of 0.8 mm comes from.
Which images can I use?
Your own, or ones you have the right to use. The program does not check what you give it, so the subject is your responsibility. No edition, Commercial included, gives you any rights to someone else's artwork.
Which pictures give the best results?
The cleaner the lines, the less there is to fix. Best of all is line art: black lines on white or on a transparent background, like a colouring page. Switch on Line Art Mode and it traces them as they are. Next come PNGs with a transparent background, such as character art: the outline is already there. Then drawings and renders with clear edges on a plain background, which Remove background clears in a few clicks. Photos, soft shading and busy backgrounds work too, but expect more time in Touch up.
Can I sell what I print?
With the Commercial edition, yes: the objects you print, made to order included. In both editions you may not sell or share the STL and SVG files themselves, nor sell objects made from the sample drawings that come with the program. When you sell what you print you are its maker, so its safety and labelling are up to you.
Windows says it protected my PC. What now?
That is SmartScreen. Windows shows it for downloads it has not seen many times yet, and every new release of a small program starts there. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Download the setup only from this page.
What if it turns out not to be for me?
That is what the trial is for: try it before you pay. After a purchase the Terms of sale apply.
